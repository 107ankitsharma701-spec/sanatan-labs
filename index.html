What we make

Sanatan Labs is an independent Indian software developer. We build devotional and reference apps for Android, made for Indian users first.

Gita Shlok Path (गीता श्लोक पथ)
All 700 verses of the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita, with audio recitation, Hindi and English meanings, and word-by-word explanations. Fully offline. No advertising. No tracking.

What the app offers

  • All 18 chapters and 700 verses — Sanskrit text with Devanagari and Roman transliteration
  • Audio recitation of every verse, with speed control, ±10 second seek and a sleep timer
  • Hindi and English meanings, plus word-by-word (शब्दार्थ) explanations
  • Listen to a single verse, a full chapter, or the entire Gita in sequence
  • Bookmarks, search, adjustable font size, light and dark themes
  • Works entirely offline — no internet connection needed to read or listen

Pricing in one line

Chapters 1 and 2 are permanently free. The remaining 16 chapters unlock with a single one-time payment of ₹49. There is no subscription and no recurring charge. See the Pricing page for details.

Where to get it

Gita Shlok Path is distributed through the Google Play Store and the Indus Appstore.
