How to pay

If you installed the app from the Google Play Store, payment is handled by Google Play Billing using the payment methods on your Google account.

If you installed it from the Indus Appstore, payment is handled by Razorpay, and you can pay by UPI, debit card, credit card, net banking or wallet. You will be asked to sign in with a Google account before paying — this is how your unlock is remembered, and how you restore it on a new phone. We do not store your email address; see the Privacy Policy for exactly what is kept.

Restoring your purchase

Your unlock is permanent and is not tied to one device.

  • Google Play version: sign in with the same Google account and the unlock restores automatically. You can also tap Settings → Restore previous purchase.
  • Indus Appstore version: tap Settings → Restore previous purchase and sign in with the same Google account you used at checkout.

If a restore does not work, write to sanatanlabs01@gmail.com and we will sort it out.
