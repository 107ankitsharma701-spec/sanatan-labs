1. Overview

Gita Shlok Path is an offline devotional app that presents all 700 verses of the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita with audio recitation, meanings and word-by-word explanations.

The short version: This app does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, phone number or location, contains no advertising, and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind. The one exception is the optional purchase: if you buy the full Gita outside Google Play, we ask for an email address, purely so you can restore your unlock on a new phone. All verse content and audio is bundled inside the app and works fully offline. Your bookmarks, listening progress and settings never leave your device.

This policy explains, plainly and completely, what the app does and does not do with information. It applies to the Gita Shlok Path Android application, however you obtained it — through the Google Play Store or the Indus Appstore.

2. Information we do NOT collect

We want to be specific rather than vague, because "we respect your privacy" means nothing on its own. The app does not collect, request, transmit or store any of the following:

  • Your name, phone number or any account credentials — the app has no sign-up, no login and no user accounts at all
  • Your location, whether precise or approximate
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or files
  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Analytics, usage statistics, crash telemetry, session recordings or behavioural profiles of any kind — the app contains no analytics SDK
  • Any advertising or marketing identifiers — the app contains no advertising SDK and displays no ads

The app requests only the INTERNET permission from Android, and uses it for exactly one purpose: contacting Google's billing servers and our purchase-verification server when you choose to make the optional in-app purchase. Reading and listening to verses requires no internet connection at all.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so that it can remember your preferences between sessions. This data lives only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded, never backed up to our servers (we operate no user database), and is never shared with anyone.

What is storedWhy
Bookmarked versesSo your saved verses are there next time you open the app
Last verse played and playback positionSo you can resume where you left off
Settings — language, theme, font size, default sleep timerSo you don't have to set them again
Unlock status of the full-Gita purchaseSo the app knows which chapters to make available
The 700 verses and their encrypted audio filesBundled with the app so it works fully offline
A crash report, if the app has crashedShown to you on the next launch so you can report the problem if you wish. It is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere automatically

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device.

4. Purchases

Chapters 1 and 2 are permanently free. The remaining chapters can be unlocked with a single optional one-time in-app purchase. This is handled entirely by Google Play Billing.

  • We never see, receive or store your card number, bank details, UPI ID or any other payment instrument. Payment is processed by Google, not by us.
  • What the app receives from Google Play is a purchase token — an opaque identifier confirming that a purchase was made. It is not linked to your name or contact details.
  • That purchase token is sent to our own verification server (see section 5) so that the app can confirm the purchase is genuine before unlocking content.
  • Refunds, payment history and receipts are managed by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. Refund requests should be made through Google Play; you may also write to us and we will help where we can.

4a. Purchases made outside Google Play (Indus Appstore)

The version of the app distributed through the Indus Appstore does not use Google Play Billing. Payment there is handled by Razorpay, a licensed Indian payment gateway.

Because there is no store account behind such a purchase, we ask you for an email address at checkout. This is the only personal information the app ever requests, and it is used for exactly one purpose: so that you can restore your unlock after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone.

  • We store a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of your email address, not the address itself. It can be matched when you type the same address again, but it cannot be read back or used to contact you.
  • Alongside it we store only the Razorpay order and payment identifiers and the amount paid, so that a purchase can be verified and restored.
  • We do not send marketing email. We do not have a mailing list. We could not add you to one even if we wanted to, because we do not keep your address.
  • Razorpay processes the payment itself and handles your card, UPI or banking details under Razorpay's Privacy Policy. We never see or store those details.

5. Our verification server

To prevent the paid content from being unlocked fraudulently, the app verifies purchases through a small server function hosted on Google Firebase Cloud Functions. This is the only server the app ever contacts other than Google's own services.

When you make a purchase, the app sends the purchase token to this function over an encrypted HTTPS connection. The function checks the token against the Google Play Developer API and replies with a simple valid / not-valid answer. As part of any internet request, the hosting infrastructure processes your device's IP address transiently. We do not maintain a user database, do not build profiles, and do not use this data for any purpose beyond validating the purchase.

Firebase is operated by Google and processes this data under Firebase's privacy terms.

6. Device integrity check

The app may use the Google Play Integrity API to confirm that it is running on a genuine, unmodified Android device and has not been tampered with. This is an anti-piracy measure that protects the paid content. Where this check is active, Google provides an integrity verdict and a device/app identifier for that purpose only. It is not used to identify you as a person, and it is not combined with any other data.

7. Audio content

The verse recitations included in the app are produced using AI voice synthesis (Vagdhenu AI) and are bundled with the app in encrypted form. The app never records audio, never requests microphone access, and never sends any audio anywhere.

8. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data with anyone — because we do not collect personal data in the first place. There are no advertising partners, no data brokers and no third-party analytics providers involved in this app.

The only third parties involved at all are Google Play Billing or Razorpay (to process the optional purchase, depending on where you downloaded the app), Google Firebase (to verify it) and the Google Play Integrity API (to protect it). Each is used strictly for the purpose described above.

9. Data retention

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Purchase records are retained by Google Play according to Google's own policies, and our verification server retains no user records.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. You may also clear the app's storage from Android Settings → Apps → Gita Shlok Path → Storage.
  • Access and correction: as we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose or correct. If you believe otherwise, write to us and we will investigate.
  • Purchase queries: refunds and payment records are handled through your Google Play account.
  • Questions: you may contact us at any time at the address below.

11. Children's privacy

This app is a general-audience devotional and reference app. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us — although, as described above, the app provides no means for anyone to do so.

12. Security

The verse audio is protected by two layers of encryption: the audio files are encrypted before being packaged into the app, and on first launch they are re-encrypted on your device using a key generated in the Android Keystore, which never leaves your device. All network requests made by the app use encrypted HTTPS connections.

No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but since the app holds no personal data, the impact of any such event on your privacy is minimal by design.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

अंतिम संशोधन: 6 सितम्बर 2026

संक्षेप में: यह ऐप आपसे कोई खाता (account) नहीं बनवाता, आपका नाम, फ़ोन नंबर या स्थान (location) नहीं माँगता, इसमें कोई विज्ञापन नहीं है, और किसी भी प्रकार की ट्रैकिंग या एनालिटिक्स नहीं है। केवल एक अपवाद है — वैकल्पिक ख़रीद: यदि आप Google Play के बाहर सम्पूर्ण गीता खरीदते हैं, तो हम आपसे एक ईमेल पता माँगते हैं, सिर्फ़ इसलिए कि नया फ़ोन लेने पर आपका अनलॉक वापस मिल सके। सभी श्लोक और ऑडियो ऐप के भीतर ही मौजूद हैं और पूरी तरह ऑफ़लाइन चलते हैं। आपके प्रिय श्लोक, सुनने की प्रगति और सेटिंग्स आपके फ़ोन से कभी बाहर नहीं जाते।

1. हम क्या एकत्र नहीं करते

  • आपका नाम, फ़ोन नंबर या कोई लॉगिन — ऐप में खाता बनाने की व्यवस्था ही नहीं है
  • आपका स्थान (location)
  • आपके संपर्क, कैलेंडर, फ़ोटो, कैमरा, माइक्रोफ़ोन या फ़ाइलें
  • विज्ञापन पहचानकर्ता (advertising ID)
  • कोई एनालिटिक्स, उपयोग-आँकड़े या व्यवहार-प्रोफ़ाइल

ऐप केवल INTERNET अनुमति लेता है, और उसका उपयोग सिर्फ़ एक काम के लिए होता है — जब आप वैकल्पिक इन-ऐप ख़रीद करते हैं, तब Google के बिलिंग सर्वर और हमारे सत्यापन सर्वर से संपर्क करने के लिए। श्लोक पढ़ने और सुनने के लिए इंटरनेट की कोई आवश्यकता नहीं है।

2. आपके फ़ोन पर क्या सहेजा जाता है

ऐप कुछ जानकारी केवल आपके फ़ोन की निजी स्टोरेज में सहेजता है, ताकि आपकी पसंद याद रहे। यह कभी अपलोड नहीं होती और किसी के साथ साझा नहीं की जाती:

  • प्रिय (bookmark) किए हुए श्लोक
  • अंतिम सुना गया श्लोक और उसकी स्थिति, ताकि आप वहीं से आगे बढ़ सकें
  • सेटिंग्स — भाषा, थीम, फ़ॉन्ट आकार, स्लीप टाइमर
  • पूर्ण गीता की अनलॉक स्थिति
  • यदि ऐप कभी बंद (crash) हुआ हो, तो उसकी रिपोर्ट — जो अगली बार आपको ही दिखाई जाती है और स्वयं कहीं नहीं भेजी जाती

ऐप को अनइंस्टॉल करने पर यह सारा डेटा आपके फ़ोन से हट जाता है।

3. ख़रीद (Purchase)

अध्याय 1 और 2 सदैव नि:शुल्क हैं। शेष अध्याय एक बार की वैकल्पिक इन-ऐप ख़रीद से खुलते हैं, जिसे पूरी तरह Google Play Billing संभालता है। आपका कार्ड नंबर, बैंक विवरण या UPI ID हम कभी नहीं देखते और न ही सहेजते हैं — भुगतान Google द्वारा संसाधित होता है। ऐप को केवल एक purchase token मिलता है, जो आपके नाम या संपर्क से जुड़ा नहीं होता। रिफ़ंड और भुगतान इतिहास Google Play के माध्यम से प्रबंधित होते हैं।

3a. Google Play के बाहर की गई ख़रीद (Indus Appstore)

Indus Appstore वाले संस्करण में भुगतान Razorpay द्वारा होता है। वहाँ कोई स्टोर खाता नहीं होता, इसलिए ख़रीदते समय हम आपसे एक ईमेल पता माँगते हैं। यह ऐप द्वारा माँगी जाने वाली एकमात्र व्यक्तिगत जानकारी है, और इसका उपयोग केवल एक काम के लिए है — ताकि ऐप दोबारा इंस्टॉल करने या नया फ़ोन लेने पर आपका अनलॉक वापस मिल सके।

  • हम आपका ईमेल पता नहीं, बल्कि उसका एकतरफ़ा हैश (SHA-256) सहेजते हैं। वही पता दोबारा लिखने पर मिलान हो जाता है, किंतु उससे आपका पता पढ़ा नहीं जा सकता।
  • साथ में केवल Razorpay के ऑर्डर/पेमेंट पहचानकर्ता और राशि सहेजी जाती है।
  • हम कोई प्रचार-ईमेल नहीं भेजते। हमारे पास कोई मेलिंग सूची है ही नहीं।
  • आपके कार्ड/UPI/बैंक विवरण Razorpay संभालता है — हम उन्हें कभी नहीं देखते।

4. सत्यापन सर्वर

ख़रीद असली है या नहीं, यह जाँचने के लिए ऐप Google Firebase Cloud Functions पर बने एक छोटे सर्वर फ़ंक्शन से एन्क्रिप्टेड HTTPS कनेक्शन द्वारा संपर्क करता है। किसी भी इंटरनेट अनुरोध की तरह, इस दौरान आपके डिवाइस का IP पता अस्थायी रूप से संसाधित होता है। हम कोई उपयोगकर्ता डेटाबेस नहीं रखते और इस जानकारी का ख़रीद-सत्यापन के अतिरिक्त कोई उपयोग नहीं करते।
