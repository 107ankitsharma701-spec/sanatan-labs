What to include

For a purchase, refund or restore problem

  • Where you installed the app from (Google Play or Indus Appstore)
  • If you paid through Indus: the Google account you signed in with — writing from that account is easiest
  • The approximate date and amount of the payment

For a bug or crash

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • What you were doing when it happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one — if the app showed an error screen, that screenshot is especially useful

For a correction in the text or meanings

  • The verse reference, for example BG.2.47
  • What is wrong, and what it should say

We have tried hard to keep the app free of errors, but scripture is vast and mistakes are possible. If you find one, please forgive us and do tell us — corrections are genuinely welcome and we act on them.
